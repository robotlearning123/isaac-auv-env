# Glider Assets Provenance

Source: DAVE (https://github.com/Field-Robotics-Lab/dave)
License: Apache-2.0
Paper: Aravind et al., "DAVE Aquatic Virtual Environment", OCEANS 2024

## Vehicles
- `slocum/` — Slocum electric glider (69.25 kg, Fossen hydro params in SDF)
- `wave_glider/` — Liquid Robotics Wave Glider (surface float + sub)
- `whoi_hybrid/` — WHOI hybrid glider with dual propellers (50 kg)

## Files per vehicle
- `mesh/` — Collada visual meshes
- `model.sdf` — Gazebo SDF with inertial + hydrodynamic params
