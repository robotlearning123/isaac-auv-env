# OceanScale v0.1 — Customer & Investor Brief

**The ocean simulator for underwater robotics.**

OceanScale is the ocean layer in the NVIDIA robotics simulation ecosystem: Isaac Sim 6, Isaac Lab 3, Newton, Warp, CUDA, and PyTorch.

---

## Page 1: The Problem & The Wedge

### The pain (from the AUV operator's desk)

- **Too few hardware test slots.** Sea trials cost $50K per dive day (vessel, crew, weather window). You get 3–5 shots per quarter, and each policy revision needs one.
- **Weeks of policy iteration.** Training a hover or docking policy on CPU-based simulators takes hours per run. Iterating through 20 candidate policies means 2–3 weeks of compute before you can even test in water.
- **The sim-to-real bridge is broken.** Existing underwater simulators (Gazebo+UUV, UWSim) use simplified hydrodynamics. Policies that converge in simulation fail at sea because the physics model doesn't match the real vehicle's drag, added mass, or Coriolis coupling.

### The wedge: GPU-scale underwater simulation

OceanScale runs Fossen 6-DOF hydrodynamics on NVIDIA Warp CUDA kernels and uses Isaac Sim 6 / Isaac Lab 3 as the current validation lane. Current launch-standardized OceanScale-only throughput on RTX 5090:

| Envs | Env-steps/s | Bench steps |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | 1,326 | 200 |
| 64 | 85,824 | 200 |
| 4096 | 4,588,922 | 200 |

Source: `benchmarks/RESULTS.md`. The older PyBullet comparison remains documented separately: OceanScale reached 17,427 env-steps/s at n_envs=64, 10.49x over single-env PyBullet on that matched CPU-baseline sweep. Do not mix the older comparison table with the launch-standardized OceanScale-only benchmark.

### Before / After workflow

**BEFORE (current state):**
- Each policy revision blocks on a sea-trial slot ($50K/dive day, 3–5 per quarter — public industry estimates).
- CPU-based simulators trade fidelity for speed; cross-coupling damping and full Coriolis are usually skipped.

**AFTER (with OceanScale):**
- Pre-sea-trial iteration moves to vectorized GPU sim — 85,824 env-steps/s at n=64 on the current launch-standardized OceanScale benchmark.
- Fossen 6-DOF + von Benzon 2022 hydrodynamics, with cross-coupling damping disabled until coefficients are independently identified.

*Cost numbers above are publicly cited industry estimates. OceanScale reduces the number of pre-sea-trial iterations needed to get a candidate policy worth testing; it does not replace sea trials. Sim-to-real transfer remains an active research problem we have not yet field-validated.*

Limitations (being honest): the bundled hover demo is a functionality smoke, not a policy-quality claim. Contact, docking, station-keeping, waypoint, and multi-vehicle code paths exist, but they are not all long-horizon release gates yet. The full official Isaac demo sweep has not been run because the OceanScale gate is the native Isaac 6 / Isaac Lab 3 environment verifier. No sim-to-real transfer validation yet. This is alpha software for design partners, not production.

### Roadmap

**v0.3 (target: Q3 2026)**
